A Hospital Tent Tensile Structure is a temporary or semi-permanent structure used to set up emergency medical facilities, testing centers, and isolation units. It provides a fast, efficient, and scalable solution for medical infrastructure.

Hospital Tents are designed for rapid deployment. They can be set up within hours, making them ideal for emergency situations like natural disasters, pandemics, or health crises.

Yes, Hospital Tents are made using PVC-coated, airtight fabric, making them waterproof, UV-resistant, and weatherproof. They are also fully air-conditioned to maintain a controlled environment for patients and staff.

These structures are used in emergency relief camps, disaster zones, military bases, hospitals, and for COVID-19 isolation units and testing centers etc.

Yes, they can be customized to meet specific medical needs, including partition walls, ventilation systems, and modular designs for patient care, testing booths, and administrative areas.
