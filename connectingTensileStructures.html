Connecting Tensile Structures:

Connecting Tensile Structures are used to link two or more buildings or spaces together, providing sheltered pathways, corridors etc. These structures are often seen in large campuses or malls, offering protection from weather while maintaining an open, airy design.

A Connecting Tensile Structure links two or more buildings, walkways, or spaces using tensile fabric canopies. It provides a covered walkway or passage that offers protection from the elements.

It provides a continuous, covered passage between buildings, ensuring easy pedestrian movement while offering protection from rain, sun, and wind. It enhances the connectivity of large complexes.

Yes, we offer customized shapes, sizes, and fabric choices. Our team works with architects to design structures that blend seamlessly with the existing building’s style and layout.

They are commonly used in airports, commercial complexes, hotels, hospitals, universities, and malls etc. to create covered walkways between buildings or different sections of a campus.

They offer faster installation, better aesthetics, natural lighting, and improved air ventilation. They are also lightweight, cost-effective, and easy to maintain compared to concrete or steel walkways.
